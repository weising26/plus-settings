Actions

Connect finds the device only while it is advertising. If every phone slot is in use, press the BOOT button, or tap “Let a phone connect” from a page that is already connected.

Android may ask to pair with “Pokemon GO Plus”. That is the device's normal Bluetooth pairing, so tap Pair.

Needs Chrome on Android. Anyone nearby can open this page while the device is advertising; there is no PIN.
