Reset pairing

Clears the saved pairing so you can pair a different phone or account. You will need to pair it again in Pokémon GO. Same as holding the D0 button for 5 seconds.

Only one phone app can connect at a time. While this page is connected, Pokémon GO can't reach the device, so tap Disconnect when you're done.

Needs Chrome on Android. If Connect can't find the settings, flash the firmware that includes the settings service.
